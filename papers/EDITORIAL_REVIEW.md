This is an empirical study of learning dynamics and interpretation of activation patching, not a state-of-the-art benchmark claim. Its strongest reader-facing lesson is that the treatment comparison changes with intervention size while the morphology contrast survives one-to-one matching. This is consistent with TMLR's emphasis on convincing evidence and clear findings of interest, rather than requiring a new state of the art.
